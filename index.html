WHEN WATER RISES,
BE READY.

Floods can happen quickly. Knowing what to do before, during and after a flood can protect you, your family and your community.

START SAFETY GUIDE ↓

🌊 FLOOD SAFETY

📢 STAY INFORMED

Follow official weather alerts and emergency instructions. Do not ignore evacuation warnings.

⬆️ MOVE HIGHER

If flooding threatens your area, move to higher ground and follow instructions from emergency authorities.

🎒 BE PREPARED

Keep essential supplies, drinking water, medicines, a flashlight and important documents ready.

🚫 AVOID FLOODWATER

Never walk, swim or drive through moving floodwater. Find a safer route.

🚨 NEVER TAKE FLOODWATER LIGHTLY

“If the water is rising, don't wait.
Get to safety.”
