⚠ FLOOD SAFETY PSA

WHEN WATER RISES,
BE READY.

Floods can happen quickly. Knowing what to do before, during and after a flood can protect you, your family and your community.

START SAFETY GUIDE ↓

🌧️ BEFORE A FLOOD

Preparation can make a major difference during an emergency.

📢 Stay Informed

Monitor official weather and flood warnings. Learn whether your area is at risk of flooding.

🎒 Prepare Supplies

Keep water, food, a flashlight, batteries, first-aid supplies and essential medicines ready.

📱 Charge Devices

Keep phones and power banks charged so you can receive emergency alerts and contact others.

🗺️ Know Your Route

Know how to reach higher ground or an official evacuation location.

📄 Protect Documents

Store important documents and valuables in waterproof containers.

🚯 Keep Drains Clear

Never throw garbage into drains. Blocked drainage can make flooding worse.

🌊 DURING A FLOOD

⬆️ Move Higher

Move to higher ground when advised and follow evacuation instructions.

🚫 Avoid Floodwater

Never walk, swim or drive through moving floodwater.

⚡ Avoid Electricity

Do not touch electrical equipment if you are wet or standing in water.

👨‍👩‍👧 Protect Others

Keep children away from floodwater and help vulnerable people follow safety instructions.

🚨 NEVER TAKE FLOODWATER LIGHTLY

Floodwater may be deeper, faster and more dangerous than it appears.

🎒 EMERGENCY KIT

Keep essential supplies ready before an emergency occurs.

💧 Drinking water
🥫 Ready-to-eat food
🔦 Flashlight and batteries
🩹 First-aid supplies
📱 Phone and power bank
💊 Essential medicines
📄 Important documents
🧼 Basic hygiene supplies
📣 Whistle

🏠 AFTER A FLOOD

✅ Wait for the All-Clear

Return home only when authorities say it is safe.

⚠️ Avoid Standing Water

Floodwater may contain sewage, debris or other hazards.

⚡ Check Electricity

Do not use electrical equipment until it has been checked and declared safe.

🍎 Check Food & Water

Use safe drinking water and discard food that may have been contaminated.

🤝 OUR MISSION

The Disaster Management Community is a new community focused on spreading awareness, preparedness and responsible action during natural disasters.


Our goal is simple: help people prepare before disaster strikes.

🌊 BE ALERT. BE PREPARED. STAY SAFE.

Don't wait for a flood to become a disaster. Prepare today.
